Roof repair

Targeted fixes for leaks, lifted shingles, damaged flashing and worn sealants.

Benefits

  • Fix the cause, not only the stain.
  • Avoid wider damage from ongoing moisture.
  • A clear scope showing what is and is not included.

Process

  1. Locate Trace the source of the problem.
  2. Quote Written repair options.
  3. Repair Materials matched where possible.

Roof replacement

A full tear-off and new roof system when repairs no longer make sense.

Benefits

  • Material options compared side by side.
  • Ventilation and underlayment reviewed as part of the scope.
  • One project plan, one point of contact.

Process

  1. Assess Measure and inspect the roof deck.
  2. Choose Select materials and colors.
  3. Install Tear-off, install, clean up.

Storm damage & inspection

A documented look at your roof after hail, wind or fallen debris.
